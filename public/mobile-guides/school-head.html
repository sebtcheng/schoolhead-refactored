01
Getting Ready

Quick Start

Installation Guide

At a Glance

To install InsightEd, open your browser and navigate to tinyurl.com/InsightEdV2. Use your browser's menu to 'Add to Home Screen' to install it as a phone-first app, allowing for seamless school reporting directly from your mobile device.

Next: Welcome to InsightEd
02
Accessing the App

Welcome to InsightEd

Portal Logic

At a Glance

Access the app by selecting the 'School Head Portal' from the main entry screen. If you are a first-time user, click 'Create Account' to begin your registration and gain access to your school’s specialized data management tools.

Next: School Registration
03
Getting Started

School Registration

At a Glance

Register your school by entering personal details, mapping your location hierarchy, and geotagging your school's exact coordinates. Remember to save your unique School ID (IERN), as it is required for all future logins.

Next: Your Dashboard
04
Manage Your School

Your Dashboard

At a Glance

Your dashboard is the central hub for school management. Use the 'CLOUD' button for comprehensive school monitoring and audits (Units 1-9), and access 'ESF7' to efficiently manage and upload your teacher personnel files.

Next: Unit 1 - School Profile
05
Your School Identity

Unit 1: School Profile

At a Glance

Confirm your school's identity, official name, and establishment dates. Upload required ownership documentation and link any mother or annex schools to ensure a complete profile.

Next: Unit 2 - Enrollment
06
Learner Count

Unit 2: Learners

At a Glance

Log enrollment by grade level and gender. The system validates that male and female counts match the total. Use the combination tool to accurately group multigrade classes.

Next: Unit 3 - Sections
07
Classroom Density

Unit 3: Sectioning

At a Glance

Organize class sections and assign instructional shifts. Categorize each section by density—Less Than, Within, or Above Standard—to monitor and maintain optimal learner-teacher ratios across your campus.

Next: Unit 4 - Profiles
08
Special Education & Health

Unit 4: Profiles

At a Glance

Record specialized data for learner communities like IP and ALS. Monitor critical health metrics and performance indicators, such as dropouts and repeaters, to provide targeted support for at-risk students.

Unit 5: Learning Modalities

At a Glance

Configure your school's learning setup by selecting between in-person, blended, or distance delivery modes. Define your shifting models and report any emergency ADM protocols to ensure accurate operational mapping for reporting.

Unit 7: Physical Facilities

At a Glance

Audit all school buildings and rooms to assess conditions. Map buildable spaces for future development and use the damage slider to report specific repair needs for prioritized maintenance.
