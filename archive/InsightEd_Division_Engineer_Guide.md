## Module 4: Infrastructure Tracking & Project Monitoring
**Objective:** Track the statuses, budgets, and accomplishment percentages of all active school building and repair projects within your Division.

### Utilizing the Engineer Dashboard:
1. **Overview Analytics:** Upon login, your active dashboard will display cards with high-level statistics:
   - **Approved Budget for Contract (ABC):** The aggregate financial allocation of all your tracked projects as archived in the master database.
   - **Projects:** The total count of active infrastructure projects.
   - **Delayed Indicator (⚠️):** Instantly highlights any projects that have passed their target completion date but have not reached 100% accomplishment.
2. **Project Status Mix:** Evaluate your division's portfolio via the interactive Pie Chart detailing the split of *Completed*, *Ongoing*, *Under Procurement*, and *Delayed* assignments.
3. **Latest Remarks:** Check the scrolling timeline for recent updates or contractor notes from active sites.

### Appraising Individual Projects:
1. **Navigate to Projects:** Tap the **[Projects]** icon (Clipboard) in the bottom navigation menu.
2. **Search & Filter:** Use the top search bar to quickly query specific schools, project names, or InsightEd Project Codes (IPC).
3. **Evaluate Progress:** Projects are sorted by priority and display a real-time progress bar. A green bar indicates a completed project, while a blue gradient indicates ongoing efforts.

## Module 5: On-Site Inspections & Updating Real-Time Status
**Objective:** Record accurate, evidence-based project updates directly from the construction or repair site.

1. **Initiate an Update:** From the Project List, locate the designated project and tap the blue **[UPDATE]** button. *(Note: Completed projects will display a locked state).*
2. **Modify Core Project Metrics:** 
   - Ensure the **Status** is accurate (*Ongoing, For Final Inspection, Completed, etc.*).
   - Update the **Accomplishment Percentage** slider or input box to reflect real-life milestones.
   - If the project is finished, ensure you declare the **Actual Completion Date**.
3. **Upload Mandatory Photographic Proof:** COA requirements mandate visual evidence for all progress updates. Scroll down to the image attachment section within the update modal.
   - Tap the **Camera Icon** to take a live photo of the site.
   - Tap the **Image/Gallery Icon** to upload an existing high-quality photo from your device's storage.
   - **Important: Categorize your evidence:** 
     - **Internal Photos:** Submit photos depicting the interior construction logic (e.g., roofing structural integrity, ceiling works, classroom finishings, electrical rough-ins).
     - **External Photos:** Submit photos proving the exterior stage of the infrastructure (e.g., foundational civil works, structural framing, exterior painting, or the complete facade).
4. **Save Your Changes:** Review all inputs. Once verified, tap **[Save]**. 
> **Important Rule:** You cannot mark a project as **[Completed]** without supplying a formal Actual Completion Date. Furthermore, progress updates without appropriately categorized photographic evidence may be rejected.

## Module 6: Maintenance & Troubleshooting
**Objective:** Resolve technical discrepancies and keep the platform optimized.

1. **Data Synchronization Hub:** Look for the **"Updates Available"** card on your dashboard. This notifies you if regional headquarters has added new projects or realignment requests to your queue.
2. **App Updates:** When prompted by the system, tap **[Reload Now]** to install vital security patches or new feature deployments over-the-air.
3. **Help Center:** Access the FAQ/Help section within the **[Profile]** menu for immediate solutions to registration queries and data parity issues.
