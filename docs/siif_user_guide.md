# School Innovation & Improvement Fund (SIIF)
## Master User Guide for School Heads

Welcome to the **SIIF Planning & Utilization Portal**. This system is designed to streamline the way you plan interventions, allocate your school's innovation fund, and track your quarterly utilization.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from logging in to submitting your final implementation plan.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Account

> [!NOTE]
> **No Registration Required!** As a School Head, your InsightED account is already registered; this is the same account you use to access the main InsightED platform.

1. Navigate to the **InsightED Nexus Portal**.
2. Log in using your official DepEd credentials (your pre-assigned email and password).
3. Once on the Nexus Dashboard, locate the **Other Services** section and click on the **SIIF Portal** icon to enter your dedicated school dashboard.

---

## 2. The SIIF Dashboard (Your Home Base)

The Dashboard gives you an instant, high-level overview of your school's financial and planning status.

### The Finance Overview
At the top of your dashboard, you will see your **Total School Allocation** for the current Fiscal Year (e.g., FY 2026).
- A real-time progress bar shows your **Utilized Budget** versus your **Remaining Balance**.
- *Note: Until you begin Phase 2 (Utilization), your utilized amount will remain at ₱0.00.*

### The Planned Interventions Master Card
Scroll down to see the beautifully designed Master Card, which summarizes your planned activities:
- **Percentage Badge (Top Right):** Shows what percentage of your official allocation your planned budget consumes. *(Warning: It will turn RED if you exceed 100% of your allocation!)*
- **Summary Metrics (Left):** Displays the **Total Learners** that you inputted in the beneficiaries section, alongside your **Combined Budget Estimate**.
- **Intervention Chips (Right):** A quick-glance list of all the interventions you plan to run (e.g., *Reading Program, Numeracy, Remediation*). 
  - **💡 Pro Tip:** Click any of the intervention chips to instantly open a detailed modal showing the specific grade levels and activities assigned to that intervention!

### Status Banners
If the Division Office reviews your submitted plan and marks it as **Disapproved**, a bright red banner will appear at the top of your dashboard detailing the specific correction instructions so you can easily revise your proposal.

## 3. The Planning Hub (Creating Your Proposal)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Deadline Enforcement:** The Planning Hub operates on a strict timeline. You can easily check the deadline at the top of your Planning Hub screen. If the deadline passes, your hub will lock into a "Read-Only" mode and you will no longer be able to submit or edit your plans.

Click the **"Modify Hub"** button on the Home page or navigate to the **Forms** tab at the bottom to start drafting your school's plan. The Planning Hub uses a smart, sequential 4-step locking system to ensure you never miss a detail.

### Step 1: Interventions
- Select the **Interventions** card to open its panel.
- Choose the specific programs you want to run in your school by ticking the circle next to them.
- Identify the specific subjects if the intervention falls under **Remediation (ARAL)**.
- Tap the **"Review Summary"** button to double-check your inputs.
- When you are satisfied with the content, type **CONFIRM** and hit the Save button to proceed.

### Step 2: Beneficiaries
- Open the **Beneficiaries** panel by selecting its card.
- For every intervention you chose earlier, input the exact number of learners you are targeting. These should be categorized cleanly by **Key Stage** (e.g., Key Stage 1: Kindergarten to Grade 3) and specific Grade Levels.
- Use the **"Review Summary"** button to view a recap of your entries.
- Once everything looks correct, type **CONFIRM** and hit Save.

### Step 3: Activities
- Access the **Activities** panel via its card.
- Select from the pre-defined, standardized activities (such as **SIP-AIP Aligned Activities**, **Action Research Activities**, or **Utilization of Remaining SIIF Balance**), and check all that apply.
- If your specific activity isn't listed, simply tick the "Others" box and type in your custom activity.
- Press the **"Review Summary"** button to review your activity selections.
- If you are satisfied with the details, type **CONFIRM** and hit Save.

### Step 4: Budget Estimation
- In this final panel, you will see a list of all the interventions you have selected so far.
- **Real-Time Total Tracker:** As you input your financial estimates, the system automatically calculates your **Total Estimate** and displays it prominently on the screen.
- **Allocation Guardrails:** The system constantly compares your Total Estimate against your School's Official Allocation limit.
  - If your budget is within the limit, the tracker indicates you are safe to proceed and shows your **Remaining Budget**.
  - If your total exceeds your Official Allocation, the tracker will instantly alert you with an **Over Limit** warning.
- **Submission Lock:** You **cannot** confirm this step or submit your overall plan if you are over budget. You must adjust your individual intervention estimates until the Total Estimate falls within your official allocated limit.
- Once your budget is balanced and finalized, select **Review Summary** and type **CONFIRM** to complete the final step of the Planning Hub.

### Submitting
- **Submit:** Once all 4 steps are confirmed, click **"View Summary"** at the bottom, review your final numbers, and hit the **Submit** button!

## 4. Utilization Tracking (Phase 2)

> [!TIP]
> **Pilot Test Notice:** If you navigate to the **Utilization** tab today, you will see an *"Under Development"* lock screen. The current pilot test focuses exclusively on the Planning Phase. Once the official **Phase 2 (Utilization tracking)** is launched, this lock will be removed. 

When Phase 2 is active, the Utilization tab becomes your primary tool for reporting actual expenditures across specific reporting periods. 

### How to Use the Utilization Tracker
- **Select Reporting Period:** Choose the current active reporting window (e.g., **July – September**, **October – December**, or **January – March**) you wish to report on.
- **Input Actual Expenditures:** For each of your approved interventions, input the **exact amount** you have spent during that specific period.
- **Update Status:** Use the dropdown menu to update the progress of each intervention (e.g., *Not Yet Started*, *Ongoing*, or *Completed*).

### Built-in Safeguards & Restrictions
The system has strict operational safeguards built-in to prevent overspending and ensure accurate reporting:
- **Reporting Period Restriction:** The entire Utilization tab is strictly locked to the specific reporting windows. You can only input and edit your expenditures during the designated months (e.g., July, October, January). If it is currently not within one of these specific reporting months, the Utilization tab will be completely disabled and locked into a "Read-Only" mode.
- **Budget Restriction:** You cannot report an expenditure that exceeds the *planned budget* for a specific intervention. If you attempt to input a higher amount, the system will block it and show you the maximum allowable remaining budget for that specific intervention.
- **Cumulative Lock:** The system automatically calculates your total spending across previous periods. If you have already exhausted the allocation for an intervention in previous periods, the system will lock the input field in the current period and alert you that the allocation is fully exhausted.
- **Max Out Warning:** If your exact input hits the budget limit for an intervention perfectly, the system will notify you that the limit is reached, reminding you that no further funds can be added to this intervention in future periods.

### Saving Your Report
- Once all expenditures are accurately recorded, click **Save Updates**. 
- The system will run a final check to ensure your *Total Utilized* across all interventions does not exceed your overall *Total Allocated Limit* before officially saving your report to the Division Office.

## 5. Printing & Reporting

Need a hard copy for your files or a presentation? 
1. Go to your **Home Dashboard**.
2. In the left section of the Master Card, click the **"Print Details"** button (printer icon).
3. The system will automatically reformat your complex data into a clean, DepEd-compliant, black-and-white table layout that looks perfect on an A4 sheet of paper!
