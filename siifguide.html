SIIF Hub User Guide

A practical guide for School Heads on preparing, reviewing, submitting, and monitoring the School Innovation and Improvement Fund plan.

Quick Rule: Finish the SIIF Hub step-by-step: Check Allocation ➔ Draft Form Sections in Order ➔ Attest & Submit ➔ Track Utilization after Approval.

Navigating InsightED

Access the official InsightED portal gateway and open the SIIF Hub module.

Video · Gateway Access

Step 1: Access the SIIF Module via InsightED Platform

  1. Go to the official link: https://stride.deped.gov.ph/insighted/

Login

Sign into your official School Head account and verify school profile details.

Video · Account Sign In

Step 1: Sign in with School Credentials

  1. Enter your official School ID.
  2. Enter your authorized Password / Passcode.
  3. Click Sign In.

Step 2: Verify School Identity & Fiscal Year

  1. Check the top bar to ensure your School Name, School ID, and active Fiscal Year are correct.
Security Warning: If the wrong school appears, log out immediately. Never enter or modify data under another school's ID.

Overview

Understand the primary navigation tabs and the complete 5-step operational workflow.

Step 1: Navigation Tabs Breakdown

Tab Primary Purpose
Dashboard Checking allocation, estimated budget, remaining funds, status, and quick actions.
Forms Preparing or revising the SIIF plan.
Utilization Recording quarterly spending and implementation progress after the plan is ready for tracking.

1. Dashboard Review
2. Forms Hub Draft
3. Summary Review
4. Submit Attestation
5. Utilization Tracking
Follow the sequential sequence in order: review allocation ceiling ➔ draft proposal ➔ confirm all sections ➔ submit for review ➔ update quarterly utilization.
D

Dashboard Tab

Review your official spending limit and proposal progress before drafting.

Video · Dashboard Overview

Step 1: Review the dashboard figures

  • Total Allocated Budget: The budget granted to your school.
  • Total Estimated Budget: The total cost of all your planned interventions.
  • Budget Utilized: The total cumulative funds spent and recorded under the utilization tab.
  • Remaining Balance: What is left from your allocation (computed from Allocation - Total Estimated Budget).
  • Forms Completion: Percentage of completed forms in the planning/forms section.
Golden Rule: Your Total Estimated Budget must never exceed the Total Allocated Budget.

Step 2: Quick Actions

  1. Plan Intervention: opens the form for creating or editing the proposal.
  2. Review Flagged Items: Takes you right to missing or incomplete sections.
  3. Export Report: Downloads or prints a summary copy of your proposal.
E

Forms Tab

The planning workspace where you draft and sequentially lock all 5 core proposal sections.

Note — Form Availability & Common Lock Messages

Check whether the form is open:

  • Look for deadline or opening schedule notices at the top of the SIIF page.
  • If the form is not yet open, prepare your data offline but wait before submitting.
  • If the form is closed, review existing data but do not expect edits to save.

Interpret common lock messages:

Lock Message Meaning / Explanation
Opening Soon / Not Yet Open The form is scheduled for a later date.
Window Closed The submission deadline has passed.
Read-Only Mode The plan can be viewed but cannot be changed.
Pending Review The plan has been submitted and is awaiting action from the Division Office.

Step 1: Understanding Sequential Locking

1. Priority Improvement Areas ➔ 2. Interventions ➔ 3. Beneficiaries ➔ 4. Activities ➔ 5. Budget Estimation

Each section prepares data for the next. You must click Confirm on each step to unlock the subsequent section.

Step 2: Proposal Status Lifecycle

Status Meaning Action Needed
Draft The school has an in-progress plan Complete and confirm all 5 sections.
Submitted Sent to Division Office for review. Wait for review or open the plan only to view it.
Reviewed Plan is officially approved. Proceed to Utilization when tracking opens.
I

Selecting Priority Improvement Areas

Forms Tab Step 1: Identify and confirm the school's primary SIP/AIP priorities.

Video · Priority Areas

Step 1: Choose Priority Improvement Areas

  1. Open Priority Improvement Areas in the Forms Hub.
  2. Select or enter the school’s most important improvement needs.
  3. Review the selected Priority Improvement Areas to make sure they align with your school's SIP/AIP development plan.
  4. Click Confirm Priority Improvement Areas to unlock Interventions.
II

Selecting Interventions

Forms Tab Step 2: Select the specific interventions your school will execute.

Video · Select Interventions

Step 1: Choose Intervention Categories

Available intervention area Purpose
Remediation, Enhancement, Enrichment Improve learning recovery, strengthen regular instruction, or extend learning.
Prevention, Wellness, Learning Environment Address early learning risks, health/readiness needs, or classroom conditions.
Acceleration, Inclusive, Engagement Support fast progress, equitable access, participation, and retention.
Behavioral, Instructional Improvement, Technology-enabled Address social-emotional barriers, teacher effectiveness, or technology-supported learning.

Step 2: ARAL Remediation Questions

  1. If choosing Remediation (ARAL), answer the ARAL tutor and subject questions.
  2. Review the selected Interventions and ensure they align with the chosen Priority Improvement Areas.
  3. Click Confirm Interventions to proceed to Beneficiaries.
III

Inputting Target Beneficiaries

Forms Tab Step 3: Enter target learner counts by grade level for each intervention.

Video · Beneficiary Counts

Step 1: Select Grade Levels

Key Stage Grades Covered
Key Stage 1 Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3
Key Stage 2 Grade 4, Grade 5, Grade 6
Key Stage 3 Grade 7, Grade 8, Grade 9, Grade 10
Key Stage 4 Grade 11, Grade 12 (SHS)

Step 2: Enter Learner Counts

  1. Enter the number of target learners per grade level.
  2. Review entered learner beneficiary counts to verify that they are realistic and correct for each intervention.
  3. Click Confirm Beneficiaries to unlock Activities.
Do not enter your total school population unless the project truly covers every single student.
IV

Selecting Activities

Forms Tab Step 4: Pick the exact activities and materials needed per intervention.

Video · Activity Selection

Step 1: Select Activity Types

Activity group Examples
School improvement and annual planning activities Learning materials, learning sessions, instructional supplies, feeding support, minor repairs, printing or reproduction.
Action research or innovation Research-based or innovative activities designed to improve learner performance.
Operational support activities Semi-expendable items, workshops, capacity-building, and other day-to-day school needs.
Other activity A clearly written activity that does not fit the listed choices.

Step 2: Confirm Activities

  1. Ensure every intervention has at least one active activity selected(If choosing Others, specify, write a clear description).
  2. Review all selected activities to ensure they are appropriate and feasible for each intervention.
  3. Click Confirm Activities to unlock Budget Estimation.
V

Inputting of Budget Estimation

Forms Tab Step 5: Encode cost estimates and ensure they stay within your allocation ceiling.

Video · Budget Entry

Step 1: Enter Realistic Costs

  1. Input the estimated budget for each intervention.
  2. No intervention should be left with a zero (0) or blank budget.

Step 2: Check Budget Ceiling

  1. Compare Total Estimated Budget with your Total Allocation.
  2. If estimated budget is higher, reduce costs or activities before submitting.
  3. Review all estimated itemized costs to ensure accuracy before confirming.
  4. Click Confirm Budget.
The system will block submission if your total estimate exceeds your school's allocation.
VI

Summary & Submission

Forms Tab Step 6: Review your consolidated proposal and execute the official submission.

Video · Final Submission

Step 1: Review the Plan Summary

  1. Click View Implementation Summary.
  2. Verify total interventions, total learners, activities, and total budget.

Step 2: Type CONFIRM & Submit

  1. Click Submit Final Plan.
  2. Type CONFIRM in the confirmation box.
  3. Click Confirm & Submit.
  4. Verify your status pill switches to Submitted.
After submission, wait for Division Office review before treating the plan as ready for utilization tracking.
F

Utilization Tab

Record actual spending and milestones once the plan is approved.

Video · Utilization Tracking

Step 1: Check Quarter Status

Quarter Period Covered
Phase 1 (Q1) July, August, September
Phase 2 (Q2) October, November, December
Phase 3 (Q3) January, February, March

Step 2: Record Spending & Status

  1. For each planned intervention, enter the amount utilized for the active quarter.
  2. Select status: Not Started, Ongoing, or Completed.
  3. If the amount is higher than the original estimate for that intervention, provide a clear justification.
  4. Click Save Quarterly Updates.
Budget Allocation Limit Note: Quarterly utilization for a specific intervention can exceed its original estimated budget as long as a clear justification is provided. However, total cumulative utilization across all interventions must never exceed the school's official total budget allocation.

Troubleshooting & Checklist

Quick solutions to common issues and pre-submission readiness checklist.

Step 1: Resolve common planning issues

Issue Likely cause Action
A section is locked A previous section has not been confirmed. Return to the earlier section and confirm it first.
Budget cannot be submitted The total estimate may exceed the allocation. Reduce or reprioritize the plan.
Beneficiaries are flagged One or more interventions have no learner counts. Add realistic target learners for each intervention.
Activities are flagged One or more interventions have no selected activity. Add at least one activity or a clear Other activity.
Utilization is unavailable The plan may still be in planning, pending review, or disapproved. Check the dashboard status before continuing.

Pre-submission and post-submission checklist

Use this final checklist before typing CONFIRM or before reporting a plan as ready for review.

Step 1: Check completeness

Step 2: Check review quality

  1. Make sure priority areas, interventions, beneficiaries, activities, and budgets tell one coherent story.
  2. Remove interventions with zero beneficiaries, zero budget, or no activities.
  3. Use remarks or Other activity text only when it adds clear review value.
  4. Print or save the report if the school needs an internal copy before submission.

Step 3: Track what happens after submission

  1. The Dashboard should show the plan as Submitted or Pending Review.
  2. If reviewed, move to utilization when the tracking period opens.
  3. When utilization opens, update only the active quarter and save quarterly updates.
Final operating rule: Move through SIIF in order: allocation review ➔ plan draft ➔ confirmed sections ➔ final submission ➔ review status ➔ utilization tracking.
