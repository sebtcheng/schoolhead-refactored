01
Getting Started

ESF7 Overview

What is ESF7?

Essential Information

The Essential School Form 7 (ESF7) is a critical national data collection tool used by DepEd to gather comprehensive personnel information about schools throughout the Philippines. ESF7 submissions ensure accurate, current staffing data registration.

Key Benefits

  • ✓ Real-time verification and validation
  • ✓ Automatic duplicate detection
  • ✓ Secure encrypted transmission
  • ✓ Complete audit trail

Critical Points

  • → Follow the official DepEd calendar for deadlines
  • → Each category must be reviewed and confirmed
  • → Updates allowed during submission period
02
Data Collection

Preparation

File Requirements

At a Glance

Prepare your personnel data in Excel (.xlsx) or CSV format with standardized column headers. Ensure all staff members are entered with complete and accurate information before uploading.

Required Personnel Categories

👩‍🏫 Teaching Staff

All classroom teachers, position, specialization, funding source

🏆 Related-Teaching Staff

LRT, Guidance Counselors, Health Nurses, Special Ed

🛠️ Non-Teaching Staff

Administrative staff, maintenance, janitorial, food service

🎯 School Head

Principal or authorized administrator information

💡
Pro Tip: Save your file with a clear name like "ESF7_[SchoolCode]_2026" for easy identification and record-keeping.
03
Upload & Process

Submission Process

10-Step Process

1

Log in to Your School Account

Access InsightEd using your School Head credentials and navigate to ESF7 Draft section.

2

Locate File Upload Area

Find the drag-and-drop zone or file browser button in the ESF7 upload interface.

3

Upload Your File

Select your prepared Excel/CSV file. System validates format automatically.

4

Monitor Progress

Watch the progress indicator. Receive unique job ticket ID for tracking.

5

Review Scan Results

Once scanning completes, review extracted personnel data for any errors.

6

Confirm Staff Categories

Click each category card to verify data. Review personnel lists carefully.

7

Mark as Reviewed

After reviewing, confirm each category with checkmark when verified.

8

Submit Registry

Once all categories confirmed, click "Submit Registry" to finalize.

9

Confirm Submission

Verification dialog appears. Confirm using required passcode or auth method.

10

Receive Confirmation

Get submission confirmation with reference number for your records.

⚠️
Important: Once submitted, ESF7 data becomes locked. If corrections needed, follow resubmission process. Keep your reference number for records.
04
Quality Control

Verification

Verification Checklist

During verification, carefully review each staff category. The system automatically validates data and flags any errors. All required fields must be complete before final submission.

What to Check

Teaching Staff

All teachers listed, positions correct, specializations accurate, funding sources valid, no duplicates

Related-Teaching Staff

LRT, Guidance, Health personnel properly classified, correct position titles and funding

Non-Teaching Staff

All support staff listed, job titles correct, funding sources accurate

School Head

Official name complete and accurate, position verified, contact information valid

05
Common Questions

FAQs

What is the deadline for ESF7 submission?

The submission deadline is set annually by DepEd. Check the official DepEd calendar or your Division Office for specific dates. Early submission is recommended.

Can I edit my ESF7 after submission?

Once submitted, direct editing is not allowed. If errors are flagged, you'll receive PENDING_RESUBMISSION status and can upload a corrected file.

What file formats are accepted?

Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files are accepted. Ensure proper column headers and save with UTF-8 encoding if using CSV format.

How long does scanning take?

Typically 2-10 minutes. For large files (500+ personnel), allow up to 15-20 minutes. Check status anytime using your job ID.
